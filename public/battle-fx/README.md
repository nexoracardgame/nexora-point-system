# Battle Effects

Textures from Kenney Particle Pack 1.1 (CC0).
Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/particle-pack
The original license is included in LICENSE.txt.

Only five transparent textures are shipped. Effects are rendered locally on a
bounded canvas, with reduced-motion support and no external runtime requests.

Audio clips from Kenney Impact Sounds (CC0):
https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
The audio license is included in audio/LICENSE.txt. Seven source clips are
converted to 22,050 Hz mono WAV for browser compatibility. Wind-up and result
tones are synthesized locally. Audio is enabled only after a user interaction;
muting and reduced-motion preferences are persisted on the device.
